# The Day Their Shadows Froze

A fantasy fiction story in progress, following Merry S. Vanier as she uncovers the hidden machinery beneath Bellweather and confronts a threat that can steal memories, silence shadows, and erase people from the world.

## Overview

This repository contains the first draft of an original fantasy novel set in the town of Bellweather. The story follows Merry, a young woman who works in a clockmaker's shop and is perpetually late to everything, as she is pulled into a mystery rooted in forgotten machinery, strange shadows, and a power capable of erasing memory itself.

## Story Summary

In Bellweather, being late is ordinary. For Merry S. Vanier, it is a habit that may soon cost her her job.

One morning, a collision with a delivery boy named Iho sends a strange black key into her pocket. The key bears the letter S and feels colder than metal should ever feel. Shortly after, her employer, Mr. Gable, becomes tangled in a growing crisis when a shadow with no body begins to move through the shop.

When a shadow that casts no body appears in the clockmaker's shop, Merry discovers that Bellweather is far stranger than she ever imagined. Shadows move of their own accord. People speak in riddles. Beneath the streets, old machines hum with a life of their own.

Merry learns that Bellweather is not simply a curious old town, but a place built over ancient mechanisms and forgotten protections. Beneath the streets lies a hidden history of clockwork, shadow-binding, and a long-lost promise made to keep the town safe.

As the mystery unfolds, Merry descends into the underground chambers beneath Bellweather and comes face-to-face with the Eraser. Rather than fighting against the key's binding force, she makes a choice that changes the shape of her destiny and the town itself.

## Characters

- **Merry S. Vanier** — The protagonist; a clock-bearer who is constantly late and suddenly tangled in a supernatural conspiracy. She possesses unusual moral courage and a willingness to question the lies hidden beneath Bellweather.
- **Meredith** — Merry's great-great-grandmother, creator of the Shadow-key.
- **Iho** — A delivery boy whose chance meeting with Merry sets the story in motion. He carries a burden of his own as the previous holder of the black key.
- **Mr. Gable** — Merry's employer and a keeper of hidden knowledge about Bellweather. He flees when the shadow appears, leaving behind cryptic warnings.
- **Cassandra / Mrs. Shadowkale** — Merry's third-degree cousin, with secrets of her own and a clearer understanding of the town's dangers. She is bound by family duty to protect Bellweather's secrets.
- **The Shadow** — A silent, hungry entity that moves in the dark and obeys forces beyond ordinary sight. It does not cast a body and responds to the power of the black key.
- **The Eraser** — A being of ancient power bound beneath Bellweather by the black key. Awakened centuries ago by Meredith, an ancestor of Merry's, the Eraser manipulates memory and shadow. Rather than being merely a monster, it is a force driven by loss and time.

## Writing Style

This story blends atmospheric world-building, mysterious tension, and supernatural suspense. It is grounded in everyday life while slowly revealing a deeper myth beneath Bellweather's streets and clockwork foundations.
